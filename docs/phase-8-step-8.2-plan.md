# Step 8.2 implementation plan: item-level refunds and settlement

Status: **plan, revision 3 (3 November 2026), awaiting the owner's authorisation. Nothing of step 8.2 is implemented.** Revision 1 was accepted as a planning document; revision 2 (commit `71d5848`) folded in the owner's six clarifications; revision 3 folds in the owner's four final points, answered in the [decision report](phase-8-step-8.2-decisions.md): a settlement-proposal table whose dispute window runs only from confirmed e-mail delivery, with reminders, lapse, bounded dispute rounds and an ADMIN decision (Q3); `bank` grants that can never be self-issued, with grant, revocation, expiry and reveal audit events (Q7); a quarantine lifecycle with named states, daily escalation from day 7 and a hard day-14 timeout, and a statement of what stays pending meanwhile (Q8, D8.19). The owner approved Q1, Q2, Q4, Q5 and Q6 on 3 November 2026 "subject to their documented safeguards"; Q3, Q7, Q8 and D8.19 are pending.

Sources: the approved [proposal](phase-8-returns-custom-artwork.md) (§3.3 R10, §4, §7.2, §8, §10.3, §11, §12, §13), the approved [decision register](phase-8-decision-register.md) (D8.5–D8.8; D8.19 pending), the [readiness document](phase-8-implementation-readiness.md) (E2, R1, R3/E5, R5, R8, R13, R15, R18) and the code landed by steps 8.0 (`93038b2`) and 8.1 (`eb1cce1`).

## 1. Scope, as approved

**In scope (proposal §13, step 8.2 "Item-level refunds and settlement: money").**

- `features/returns/refund-calculation.ts`: the pure arithmetic of §4.2–§4.3 with the deterministic per-unit rule below, and the key derivation of erratum E2.
- The settlement operation (proposal §3.3 R10): ADMIN, sensitive, under the order and request locks, from `INSPECTED` to `CLOSED`, writing `return_refund_lines` and queueing refunds through the Phase 6 engine, or recording a manual refund for cash-on-delivery orders.
- The customer-facing steps the revised decisions require before settlement: the explicit resolution choice for an exchange preference (Q1) and the settlement proposal with its dispute window (Q3).
- Tables and columns: `return_refund_lines`, `return_deductions`, `return_settlement_proposals`, `manual_refunds`, `refunds.return_request_id`, the beneficiary fields D8.8 requires, the choice timestamps and the held-unit columns; `users.grant_manager_since`; `CUSTOMER_DATA_KEY` with its rotation job (Q5); the `bank` grant scoped to one return request with its no-self-grant CHECK, and the `returns.bank_view` and `returns.bank_grant` capabilities (Q7).
- The settlement pages, the manual-refund "mark paid" operation, the "cause" column on the refund queue, the customer's refund breakdown, the new e-mails, and reports by cause.
- The staff photograph upload the deduction rule (D8.7) depends on, using the `RETURN_INSPECTION` ledger purpose that step 8.1 created but did not use.

**Out of scope, deliberately.** Exchange settlement and replacement orders (8.3; the request form stops offering exchanges until then); lost-in-transit refunds, inspection corrections and held-stock release UI beyond what D8.19 needs (8.4); commissions (8.5–8.8); any change to the refund worker, the Razorpay adapter, checkout or inventory arithmetic.

### 3.3 Pure calculation (`features/returns/refund-calculation.ts`)

- `allocateDiscount(lines, discountInPaise)`: `share_i = floor(D × L_i / S)`; the remainder goes one paisa each to the largest fractional parts, ties by ascending variant id; shares sum exactly to `D`; `discounted_i = L_i − share_i`.
- `unitValues(discounted, q)`: `unit = floor(discounted / q)`, `r = discounted − unit × q`; units 1..(q − r) are worth `unit`, units (q − r + 1)..q are worth `unit + 1`; `value(k) = k × unit + max(0, k − (q − r))`, `value(q) = discounted`.
- `partialValue(discounted, q, alreadyRefundedUnits u, k)` = `value(u + k) − value(u)`: returned units are always valued from unit 1 upward, continuing where earlier settlements on the same line stopped (`u` = Σ `units` of earlier `ITEM_VALUE` lines for the order item). Partial returns never exceed the average per unit; sequential partials reconcile exactly to `discounted`.
- `acceptedUnits(item, policy, choices)`: restock + repair (+ quarantine when `heldUnits.refundQuarantineAtSettlement`, else deferred per D8.19) + write-off when the reason is a fault reason or `refundWrittenOff` was chosen; never `return_to_customer` or `not_received`; an item with an unconfirmed exchange preference contributes nothing and blocks settlement (Q1).
- `deductionCeiling(G)` = `floor(G × maxPercent / 100)`; `validateDeductions(line)`: each reason once, each amount ≥ 1, evidence present, condition compatible, no fault reason, combined sum ≤ ceiling (Q3).
- `buildBreakdown(input)`: ITEM_VALUE per item, one DAMAGE_DEDUCTION per deduction row, SHIPPING_REFUND (`H` when the shop is at fault or every unit of the order is accepted across closed settlements), COD_FEE (0), RETURN_SHIPPING_CHARGE (only when a shop label was used and the policy amount is positive), then caps FLOOR_ZERO, CAP_PROVIDER_BALANCE, CAP_ORDER_VALUE, each adding a negative line when it bites. Output: lines and `refundTotalInPaise ≥ 0`.
- `splitAcrossAttempts(total, attempts)` and `deriveOperationKey(settlementKey, label)` (RFC 4122 version 5, erratum E2) as in revision 1.

### 3.4 Services

**Customer (`features/returns/service.ts`).**

- `confirmResolution(user, requestNumber, itemId, choice)`: `REFUND` or `WAIT`, allowed only after inspection and while the item has an exchange preference; recorded with a timestamp; reversible until settlement; event and e-mail (Q1).
- `respondToProposal(user, requestNumber, proposalNo, { accept } | { dispute, message })`: only on the request's latest proposal while it is `SENT`; an acceptance sets `ACCEPTED` with `outcome_at`; a dispute (message of at least 10 characters) sets `DISPUTED`, writes a message row and alerts the owner; both are explicit, rate-limited, request-scoped actions (Q3). Rendering the return page while a proposal is `SENT` stamps `viewed_at` once (evidence, never consent).
- `saveReturnBeneficiary(user, requestNumber, input)` for COD orders only, encrypted at once (D8.8, Q5).
- Views expose the proposal (breakdown lines and photographs), the choice and dispute state, refund lines and refund statuses per §4.5, the manual refund state; never more than the last four characters of bank details.

**Admin (`features/admin/returns/settlement.ts`, under `features/admin` so the signature scan covers it).**

- `proposeSettlement(ctx, id, input, now)`: `returns.settle` (sensitive); validates decisions (every item once; deductions per Q3; written-off choices per Q8; shop label flag); computes the breakdown under the lock; inserts the next `return_settlement_proposals` row (`SENT`) and the outbox row `return.settlement_proposed` (dedupe key per proposal number) in the same transaction, marking an open previous proposal `SUPERSEDED`; a revision after a dispute is the same operation and counts a round, refused beyond `maxDisputeRounds` unless every deduction drops to zero; audited `return.settlement_proposed`. Required whenever any deduction is present; optional otherwise.
- `resendProposal(ctx, id, proposalNo)`: `returns.settle`; only for an `UNDELIVERED` proposal; queues a fresh outbox row for the same proposal (the window will run from its delivery); audited `return.settlement_proposal_resent`.
- `decideSettlement(ctx, id, proposalNo, { deductions, reason })`: `returns.settle` (sensitive); allowed only when the latest proposal is `DISPUTED` and an earlier proposal of the request was disputed too (rounds exhausted); amounts at or below the latest proposal; reason of at least 20 characters; sets `DECIDED` with `decided_by_id`; e-mails `return.settlement_decided`; audited `return.settlement_decided`.
- `settleReturn(ctx, id, input, now)`: `enforceActorLimit(settlePerActor)`; `runAudited` (`return.settled`, `returns.settle`); transaction: `lockOrder` → `lockReturnRequest` → re-read; replay by `settlement_operation_key` (same key returns the stored summary; another key on a settled request is refused); status guard `INSPECTED`; **preconditions**: no item with an unconfirmed exchange preference (Q1); the latest proposal is `ACCEPTED`, `DECIDED`, or `LAPSED` with `delivered_at` set and its outbox row re-read as `SENT`, or has no deductions (Q3); the submitted decisions equal that proposal's snapshot (otherwise re-propose); recompute the breakdown under the lock with `refundableAttempts`; write deductions (`return_deductions` rows and the line totals), `refund_written_off`, `return_refund_lines`; **online**: `queueRefundIntent` per attempt with `reason: "customer_return"`, derived keys, `returnRequestId`; **COD**: `manual_refunds` row `PENDING` with the request's beneficiary (refused `BENEFICIARY_MISSING`); quarantined units get `hold_state = HELD` with `hold_decision_due_at` and `hold_deadline_at` (and, under D8.19's recommended value, no refund line yet); `moveTo(CLOSED)` with `settled_at`, `closed_at`, `settlement_operation_key`; return event, order events per refund, outbox `return.closed`; audit `after` with totals, the proposal number and the **basis** (`ACCEPTED`, `LAPSED`, `DECIDED`, `NO_DEDUCTIONS`) only.
- `resolveHeldUnit(ctx, id, itemId, outcome)` (`returns.inspect`, audited `return.held_resolved`, conditional on `hold_state IN (HELD, OVERDUE)`): `RELEASED` → `applyAdjustment` restock and, under D8.19's recommended value, the unit's refund queued through the engine with the derived key `held:<itemId>` (plus the shipping line when the all-units rule becomes true); `WRITTEN_OFF` (reason required) → held-stock write-off and a refund only for a fault reason or by recorded ADMIN choice; `SENT_BACK` → no refund; e-mail `return.held_resolved`. The returns clock job moves `HELD → OVERDUE` at `hold_decision_due_at` with a daily owner alert (deduplicated per unit and day), and at `hold_deadline_at` resolves the unit itself as `TIMED_OUT` / `ACCEPTED_BY_TIMEOUT`: write-off `QUARANTINE_TIMEOUT`, the refund under the same derived key, e-mail `return.held_timed_out`, audit `return.held_timed_out` with a system actor, owner alert. Under the proposal's value the same lifecycle runs for the stock only.
- `markManualRefundPaid`, `cancelManualRefund` (`returns.manual_refund`, sensitive) as revision 1.
- `issueBankGrant(ctx, input)` / `revokeBankGrant(ctx, grantId, cause)` (`features/admin/customers/service.ts`): `returns.bank_grant` (sensitive), which `requirePermission` resolves as the ADMIN role or `users.grant_manager_since IS NOT NULL`, read live with the context; the grant names one return request, one grantee (an enrolled, verified staff account other than the issuer), a reason and an expiry of at most 24 hours (default `bankGrantDefaultHours`); self-grant, an unenrolled grantee, an over-long expiry or a missing reason are refused with a REFUSED row `grant.bank.refused`; `enforceActorLimit(grantPerActor)`; revocation by the issuer, any grant manager or ADMIN is immediate; both write `grant.bank.issued` / `grant.bank.revoked` and an owner notice. A role change or `admin:grant-manager --remove` revokes the bank grants the person holds and issued. The daily sweep writes `grant.bank.expired` once per lapsed grant with the reveals it served. Script `npm run admin:grant-manager -- <email> [--remove]` sets or clears the designation (verified, enrolled account only; `script:` audit row; refuses production without confirmation, like `admin:promote`).
- `revealBeneficiary(ctx, manualRefundId, reason)`: requires an active `bank` grant for `ctx.user` on this return request (re-read `FOR SHARE`), a fresh second factor, the explicit reveal action; writes a `staff_pii_reveals` row (`fields = ['bank']`, ten minutes) and `return.bank.revealed`; counts against `revealPerActor` (Q7). STAFF without a grant and ADMIN without a grant both get a refusal with a REFUSED audit row `return.bank.reveal_refused`.

**Keys (`server/security/customer-data.ts`, job `rotate-customer-data-key`).** `encryptCustomerSecret`/`decryptCustomerSecret` with `CUSTOMER_DATA_KEY` (version n) and `CUSTOMER_DATA_KEY_PREVIOUS` (n − 1); the job re-encrypts rows with `beneficiary_key_version < n` in batches of 100 with a conditional update per row, is idempotent and resumable, reports counts, writes one system audit row per run, and lists undecryptable rows by id without changing them (Q5). Env schema: both optional in development and tests; in production `CUSTOMER_DATA_KEY` required when `checkoutPolicy.cod.enabled`.

**Staff evidence.** `issueStaffUploadTicket(ctx, "RETURN_INSPECTION", returnRequestId)` and `verifyStaffUpload` as revision 1; the file route already serves attached rows to the request's owner and to `returns.view` holders.

## 4. Transactions, idempotency, concurrency, audit, recovery

- **One transaction** per settlement under `orders → return_requests` locks with refund reads; no inventory (stock moved at inspection) and no second order (replacement orders are 8.3). Proposals, choices and disputes are their own short transactions under the same locks.
- **Idempotency**: settlement key on the request (unique); per-attempt refund keys as version-5 UUIDs derived from it (E2); replay returns the stored summary; another key on a settled request is refused; `queueRefundIntent` and `refunds.operation_key` are the backstops. A held-unit release uses a derived key `held:<itemId>` so a repeated release cannot refund twice.
- **Preconditions are re-checked under the lock**: no unconfirmed exchange item; proposal acceptance or lapsed window with no open dispute; decisions equal to the snapshot; caps from `refundableAttempts`.
- **Concurrency**: two settlements of one request serialise on the request lock and the second fails the status guard; a settlement racing a goodwill refund serialises on the order lock; a customer dispute racing a settlement is resolved by the lock (whichever commits first wins; a settlement that commits first already had the window lapsed, so the late dispute is recorded as a post-settlement message and alerts the owner).
- **Audit**: `return.settlement_proposed`, `return.settlement_proposal_resent`, `return.settlement_proposal_undelivered` and `return.settlement_proposal_lapsed` (system actor), `return.settlement_decided`, `return.settled` (totals, proposal number and basis), `return.held_resolved`, `return.held_timed_out` (system actor), `manual_refund` rows for payment and cancellation, `grant.bank.issued` / `refused` / `revoked` / `expired`, `return.bank.revealed` / `reveal_refused`; customer actions (accept, dispute, choice) are return events and message rows; `return_refund_lines`, `return_deductions` and `return_settlement_proposals` immutable or append-only; allow-lists exclude free text and beneficiary data.
- **Failure recovery**: provider failures stay with the worker (retries, `FAILED` with alert, `retryRefund`, external marking); unpaid manual refunds alert daily from day 3 and escalate at day 10; a crash after commit is a replay on resubmission; the rotation job is resumable.

## 7. Acceptance criteria (measurable)

1. A settlement of an online order queues refunds whose sum equals the breakdown total, never exceeds the captured balance under any combination in the test matrix, and whose rows carry `return_request_id`, reason `customer_return` and version-5 keys derived from the settlement key.
2. The same settlement submitted twice with the same key produces one set of refund rows and lines; another key on a settled request is refused; concurrent settlements produce one result.
3. No item with an unconfirmed exchange preference can be settled; the customer's choice (refund or wait) is recorded, reversible until settlement, reminded at 3, 7 and 14 days, and the request is marked on hold after 30 days with no automatic conversion.
4. Every deduction has a reason from the list, at least one staff photograph from the request, a compatible condition, is never on a fault line, and the combined deductions of a line never exceed 30 % of the accepted units' value; a settlement with deductions requires the latest proposal to be accepted, lapsed after confirmed delivery (a test forces the outbox row to `FAILED` and proves the proposal becomes `UNDELIVERED` and settlement stays refused however much time passes), or decided after two dispute rounds; the settlement audit row names the basis and the proposal number.
5. Per-unit values follow the deterministic rule: for every `q` ≤ 10, every `r` and every sequential split of partial returns, the partial values are non-decreasing in steps of `unit` or `unit + 1` and reconcile exactly to the discounted line value; Appendix A and the decision report's examples 1–10 pass as fixtures.
6. A COD settlement creates one pending manual refund with encrypted details and no `refunds` row; a `bank` grant can be issued only by ADMIN or a grant manager, never to themselves (refused by the service and by the CHECK under direct SQL), only for one request and at most 24 hours; reveal requires that grant, a fresh second factor and an explicit action, and writes an audit row; revocation masks on the next render; expiry writes an audit row; mark-paid is conditional, audited and e-mailed; unpaid rows alert daily from day 3 and escalate at day 10; ciphertext is nulled after retention; the rotation job re-encrypts every row and reports zero candidates afterwards.
7. The worker processes settlement refunds end to end through the fake gateway, including a lost response and a non-retryable refusal; a failed refund leaves the request closed and the customer page states the delay.
8. The customer page shows the proposal, the breakdown and each refund's state; the admin queue shows the cause; reports show refunds by cause, manual refunds and held units, from real rows only.
9. Every audit row for proposal, settlement, release, payment, cancellation, grant and reveal exists with the right outcome and no beneficiary data.
10. No quarantined unit stays held past `quarantineMaxDays`: the job marks `OVERDUE` at the due date with a daily alert and resolves `TIMED_OUT` at the deadline with the write-off, the refund under the derived key (under the recommended D8.19 value) and the customer e-mail; a repeated run changes nothing; the shipping refund is produced at the first settlement where D8.6's condition is decidable.
11. The complete gate passes locally and in CI; the Phase 6 and 8.1 suites run unchanged; migration and rollback rehearsed on a scratch database in the order 8.2 then 8.1.
